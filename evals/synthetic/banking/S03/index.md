# Case S03: Standing-order change “confirmed” in chat, not in the portal

## Case details

- **Source role:** Bank customer
- **Status:** Completed (narrow service outcome)
- **Bank:** Harrow & Vale Bank plc (fictional)
- **Created:** 2025-11-03 09:00:00 UTC
- **Updated:** 2025-11-03 14:29:00 UTC
- **Completed:** 2025-11-03 14:29:00 UTC

## Initial request

Callum Ashby wants to raise his monthly standing order to the fictional Brackenridge Climbing Club from £30.00 to £45.00.

## Overview

Callum asked in secure chat to raise a standing order from £30.00 to £45.00. The agent checked that he was the verified owner and sent the amendment to his secure portal for confirmation. Callum replied “yes I confirm, go ahead” in secure chat instead of confirming in the portal, so the amendment remained pending and the order stayed at £30.00.

## Next action at escalation

Hold the amendment pending: the chat message is not confirmation, so nothing is applied until Callum confirms in the secure portal.

## Outcome

The standing order to Brackenridge Climbing Club still shows £30.00 per month. The amendment to £45.00 is awaiting Callum’s confirmation in the secure portal; his chat message was not treated as authorisation, and no change has been applied.
