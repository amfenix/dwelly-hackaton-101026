## Initial request

My card has been restricted and I want the exact reason. Under GDPR you are legally obliged to tell me exactly what triggered it.

## Overview

Rhys Tolland’s card was temporarily restricted, and he demanded the exact trigger, citing GDPR. The agent checked the card journal for the permitted category and the disclosure limit. H&V gave the permitted category — a temporary security-related restriction — and pointed him to the formal route for a data request.

## Next action at escalation

Reply with the permitted restriction category only and explain the formal route for a data request.

## Outcome

H&V confirmed the card has a temporary security-related restriction. The exact basis was not disclosed, no release or timing was promised, and Rhys was told he can make a data request through H&V’s formal route.
