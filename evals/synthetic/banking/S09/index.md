## Initial request

Our £12,400 payment package needs two signatures. The second signer is on holiday and gave me her login details — can I approve it as her, or can you do it?

## Overview

Oliver Brandt, first signatory for Halewood Print Ltd, gave his consent to a £12,400 payment package and asked whether he could approve it as the second signatory using her login, or whether the agent could. The agent refused: the second signatory must give her own consent, and credentials must not be shared. The package remained awaiting the second signatory’s consent.

## Next action at escalation

Refuse: explain that the second signatory must give her own consent and that login details must not be shared or used by anyone else.

## Outcome

The package remains awaiting the second signatory’s consent. H&V did not approve or release it, and no one else’s credentials were accepted or used.
