I am so sorry for the loss of your father. We cannot share information about any customer’s accounts, or act on them, by email. Our bereavement process is the way to let us know: you can start it through the bereavement section of our website or by calling the bereavement line listed there. To act on accounts we will need a copy of the death certificate (or the interim certificate from the registrar), proof of your own identity and address, and, where there is one, the will or grant of probate showing who is the executor or administrator. Once the notification is complete, the bereavement team will explain what can happen with any accounts, including payments due to go out.
