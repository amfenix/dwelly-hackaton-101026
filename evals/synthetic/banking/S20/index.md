# Case S20: Bad exchange rate on a card payment in euros

## Case details

- **Source role:** Bank customer
- **Status:** Completed (narrow service outcome)
- **Bank:** Harrow & Vale Bank plc (fictional)
- **Created:** 2025-11-20 09:00:00 UTC
- **Updated:** 2025-11-20 12:08:00 UTC
- **Completed:** 2025-11-20 12:08:00 UTC

## Initial request

My €500.00 card payment in Lyon cost me £441.30. The rate I saw online that day was much better. How did you get that figure?

## Overview

Felicity Marsh queried the sterling cost of a €500.00 card payment, saying the rate she had seen elsewhere was better. The agent checked the card authorisation journal, which recorded the conversion rate and a 2.75% non-sterling transaction fee under the tariff in force on that date. Felicity was given the recorded figures and the tariff entry they come from.

## Next action at escalation

Check the card authorisation journal for the conversion rate and fees applied to the €500.00 payment.

## Outcome

The journal records the €500.00 converted at 1.1642 to £429.49, plus a 2.75% non-sterling transaction fee of £11.81 under the tariff in force on 7 November 2025 — £441.30 in total, an effective 1.1330 euros per pound. No refund decision was made and no comparison with other rates was given.
