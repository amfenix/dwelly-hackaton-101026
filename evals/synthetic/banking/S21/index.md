## Initial request

I paid a £350 cheque in through the app two days ago and it still isn’t in my balance. Where is it?

## Overview

Imogen Thackeray asked in secure chat about a £350 cheque she paid in through the H&V app two days earlier. The agent checked H&V’s cheque clearing journal, which recorded the cheque as received and in clearing, with an expected clearing day. No statement was made that the funds are available or that the cheque will be paid.

## Next action at escalation

Check the cheque clearing journal for the status of Imogen’s £350 app deposit.

## Outcome

The cheque clearing journal recorded the £350 cheque as received on 19 November 2025 and in clearing, expected to clear by the end of the next working day (Monday 24 November 2025). The funds were not confirmed as available, and H&V gave no assurance that the cheque will not be returned unpaid.
