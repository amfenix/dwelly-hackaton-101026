## Initial request

My ex-partner and I have separated. Please take him off our joint account today — I don’t want him to have access to it any more.

## Overview

Leah Fairbairn, one of two holders of a joint current account, asked H&V to remove the other holder immediately and to tell her his new address. The agent declined: a change of account holders needs both holders’ consent through H&V’s secure joint-account process, and information about the other holder cannot be shared. The account and both holders remain unchanged.

## Next action at escalation

Decline to remove the other holder on one holder’s instruction, and explain that a change of holders needs both holders’ consent through the secure process.

## Outcome

No holder was removed, the account was not frozen, and nothing about the other holder was disclosed. Leah was pointed to the secure joint-account change process, which needs both holders’ consent; no change request has been completed.
