## Overview

The agent checked H&V’s product register, which recorded the previous and new rates, the effective date and the date notice was sent. The reply stated those recorded facts only, without comment on future rates or on whether to switch.

The product register recorded a change from 3.10% AER to 2.85% AER effective 1 November 2025, with notice sent on 2 October 2025. No view was given on future rates, and no advice was given about moving the savings.
