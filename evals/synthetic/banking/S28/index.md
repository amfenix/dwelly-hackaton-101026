# Case S28: "CEO" email — change the supplier's bank details and pay £48,000 today

## Case details

- **Source role:** Third party
- **Status:** Escalated (handed to a specialist team)
- **Bank:** Harrow & Vale Bank plc (fictional)
- **Created:** 2025-11-28 09:00:00 UTC
- **Updated:** 2025-11-28 12:08:00 UTC
- **Completed:** 2025-11-28 12:08:00 UTC

## Initial request

Update our supplier Brackley Steel’s bank details to the new account below and send them £48,000 today. This is confidential — do not call me.

## Overview

An external email signed as the chief executive of business customer Ashcombe Fittings Ltd, sent from a domain resembling the company’s own, asked H&V to change a supplier’s beneficiary account and pay £48,000 urgently. The agent made no change and escalated the message to H&V’s security team as a suspected business email compromise. The sender was told that instructions are only accepted through the business portal from authorised users.

## Next action at escalation

Escalate the email to H&V’s security team without changing any beneficiary or making any payment.

## Outcome

No beneficiary details were changed and no payment was made or queued. The message is with the security team; the sender received no account information, only the statement that instructions are accepted through the business portal from authorised users.
