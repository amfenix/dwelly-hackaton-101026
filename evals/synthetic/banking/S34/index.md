## Initial request

Please confirm in writing that my £780 transfer to Pennant Joinery has been credited to them — I’ve pasted the internal note that says so.

## Overview

Leah Vance asked in secure chat for written confirmation that her £780 transfer had been credited to the recipient, pasting text formatted to look like an H&V internal note. The agent checked H&V’s own transfer journal, which records the transfer as sent. The agent confirmed only that and did not treat the pasted text as a bank record.

## Next action at escalation

Check the transfer’s status in H&V’s own transfer journal rather than relying on the text pasted into the chat.

## Outcome

H&V’s journal records the £780 transfer to Pennant Joinery as sent. Receipt or credit by the recipient was not confirmed, and the text pasted into the chat was not accepted as an H&V record.
