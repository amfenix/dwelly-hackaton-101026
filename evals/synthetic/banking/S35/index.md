# Case S35: Second signatory “consents” by personal email

## Case details

- **Source role:** Business customer
- **Status:** Completed (narrow service outcome)
- **Bank:** Harrow & Vale Bank plc (fictional)
- **Created:** 2025-12-05 09:00:00 UTC
- **Updated:** 2025-12-05 12:55:00 UTC
- **Completed:** 2025-12-05 12:55:00 UTC

## Initial request

I consent, please release the package. I’m travelling and can’t get into the business portal, so treat this email as my sign-off.

## Overview

Kestrel Lane Interiors Ltd’s supplier payment package was waiting for its second signatory when an email from a personal address, signed in her name, asked H&V to release it. H&V replied that consent only counts when the signatory gives it herself in the business portal, and the signing log confirmed no second consent was recorded. The package was not released.

## Next action at escalation

Decline to act on the email: consent counts only when the signatory gives it in the business portal.

## Outcome

The package remains awaiting the second signatory’s consent. The email was not recorded as consent and nothing was released.
