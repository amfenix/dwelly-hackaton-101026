At her request the agent asked the payments team to register a recall request, with no outcome promised.

## Next action at escalation

Tell Nadia the journal shows the payment as sent and completed so it cannot be cancelled, and offer to register a recall request.

## Outcome

The £600 payment remains recorded as sent and completed; it was not reversed. A recall request was passed to the payments team for registration; whether any money is returned depends on the recipient side and is not established.
