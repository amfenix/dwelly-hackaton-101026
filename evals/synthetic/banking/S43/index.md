## Initial request

My supplier Kestrel Joinery says they never received the £310 I paid them on 4 November. Did they get paid?

## Overview

Tomasz Whitcombe asked whether his £310 transfer to Kestrel Joinery reached the supplier. The transfer journal recorded it as sent on 4 November, and the incoming-entry journal recorded £310 credited back to his account on 6 November as returned by the beneficiary bank. Both facts were reported separately.

## Next action at escalation

Reply to Tomasz with both recorded facts: the transfer was sent on 4 November and £310 was returned and credited back on 6 November, without stating that the supplier was paid.

## Outcome

H&V’s records show the £310 transfer as sent on 4 November 2025 and a £310 credit back to Tomasz’s account on 6 November 2025 recorded as “returned by beneficiary bank”. The record does not show the supplier as paid, and no reason for the return is recorded beyond that wording.
