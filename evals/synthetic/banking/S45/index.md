## Initial request

Can you tell me where my €2,600 transfer to Atelier Rousset in Bordeaux has got to?

## Overview

Henrietta Marsh asked about an international €2,600 transfer on 15 December and was told the journal showed it pending with a downstream bank. On 18 December she asked again; rather than repeating the three-day-old status, the agent asked H&V’s payment team for a fresh trace, which recorded the payment as released onward to the beneficiary bank on 17 December.

## Next action at escalation

Ask H&V’s payment team for a fresh trace of the €2,600 transfer instead of repeating the 15 December status.

## Outcome

The fresh trace of 18 December records the transfer as released by the correspondent bank to the beneficiary bank on 17 December 2025. Credit to Atelier Rousset’s account is not confirmed by H&V’s records. The 15 December “pending” status was not given as current.
