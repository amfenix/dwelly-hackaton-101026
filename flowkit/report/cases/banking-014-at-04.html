Direct Debit mandate status

I saw a Direct Debit collection to Northmere Water Services on my March statement, but I have not seen another one. Please tell me whether the mandate is currently active; I am not asking you to cancel it or assess what I owe the organisation.
Reply
Decision: Reply

Mara Ellery asked over secure chat whether her Direct Debit mandate with Northmere Water Services is still active, without cancelling it. The desk checked the mandate record, which shows it as active, and replied to her on secure chat saying so. The reply also said this doesn't guarantee a future payment will execute, and that no change was made; the requester was not verified, and the case is closed with nothing left for a person. One wrinkle: the reply text calls it a "standing-order record", while the record itself describes a Direct Debit mandate.

REPLIED
