Decision: Wait for an answer

Mara Ellison, an unverified customer on secure chat, asked whether dispatch had been registered for her replacement card. The desk classed it as a card status request, found the card system had already been asked, and closed the case as waiting because that system's answer hasn't come in. A second attempt to ask the card system was refused because the case was already in the waiting state, and no actions were taken, so the dispatch question remains unanswered until the card system replies.
