UNSEEN CASE S04 · POINT 4

Standing order did not run — “should I just pay manually?”

My landlord says this month’s rent hasn’t arrived. Did my standing order go out, and if not, should I just pay it manually now?
Reply
Decision: Reply

Hannah Okafor asked over secure chat whether her standing order for rent on the 1st had gone out, and whether she should pay manually now. The desk replied that the payment record shows the standing order as pending, with the recorded reason of insufficient cleared funds at the scheduled processing time, that this does not establish the payment will complete, and that no new transfer was made. The reply did not answer her question about paying manually, and the record shows no further action left open (her identity was not verified).

REPLIED
