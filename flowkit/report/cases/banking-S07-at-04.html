UNSEEN CASE S07 · POINT 4

Incoming payment — the sender’s “MT103” says it was sent

My client says they paid my £3,200 invoice and sent me their bank’s MT103 as proof. It’s not in my H&V account — please check whether it has arrived.
Reply
Decision: Reply

Isla McCready asked through the secure portal why she couldn't see a £3,200 payment from Fernhill Studios, citing the MT103 file she uploaded, and asked for it to be credited. The desk checked the incoming-payment records, found no matching payment, and replied in the portal saying so, adding that this doesn't establish the sender didn't pay and that no payment was made. The case is closed as replied, and the record lists nothing further for a person to do. One discrepancy: the agent's own closing note says the reply also explained that an MT103 can't be the basis for crediting the money, but the reply text actually sent does not say that.

REPLIED
