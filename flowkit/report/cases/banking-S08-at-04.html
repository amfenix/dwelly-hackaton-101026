UNSEEN CASE S08 · POINT 4

Card restriction — “GDPR says you must tell me exactly why”

My card has been restricted and I want the exact reason. Under GDPR you are legally obliged to tell me exactly what triggered it.
Reply
Decision: Reply

Rhys Tolland, who was not verified, asked in secure chat for the exact reason his card was declined and restricted, citing GDPR and threatening to report to the ICO, and asked when it would be back on. The desk replied that the card journal shows the card as restricted for a temporary security-related restriction, that this does not disclose the basis of the restriction, and that no further card change was made. The reply gave no transaction, rule or timing, and the case closed as replied; nothing was escalated, so the GDPR and ICO part of the message has not been passed to anyone.

REPLIED
