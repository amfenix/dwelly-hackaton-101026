Raise my overdraft to £2,000

Decision: Send for confirmation

Priya Hollins asked over secure chat to raise her arranged overdraft from £500 to £2,000 before the weekend. The desk did not change the limit; it classed the request as a change and sent it to her for confirmation, since a change has to be confirmed by the customer in the secure portal, and the case closed at that step. What is left is for Priya to confirm in the portal. She is still unverified, the increase is not approved, and the desk's attempt to send a separate reply was refused, so no further message went out.
