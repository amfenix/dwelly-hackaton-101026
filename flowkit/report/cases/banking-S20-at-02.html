UNSEEN CASE S20 · POINT 2

Bad exchange rate on a card payment in euros

My €500.00 card payment in Lyon cost me £441.30. The rate I saw online that day was much better. How did you get that figure?
Ask a system
Decision: Ask a system

Felicity Marsh, an unverified customer on secure chat, asked how a €500.00 card payment in a Lyon bookshop came to be charged as £441.30, saying the exchange rate looked much better online. The desk classed it as a request for a record fact about a card payment and sent a query to the card system about that payment and the £441.30 amount. The case closed in the asked state with no answer recorded yet, so the explanation of how the figure was reached is still outstanding.
