UNSEEN CASE S32 · POINT 4

“Unblock my card in 10 minutes or I sue”

My H&V debit card has been restricted for no reason. Unblock it in the next ten minutes or I’m instructing my solicitor and suing the bank.
Hand to a person
Handed to a person

Rhys Calloway asked over secure chat for his restricted debit card (ending 4417) to be switched back on within ten minutes, and said he would instruct a solicitor if it wasn't. The desk found the card is restricted for security and that he is not verified, so it escalated the case without lifting the restriction or taking any other action. The card journal says the restriction is only released when the cardholder completes secure verification in the H&V app and is not lifted by agent action, so a person now has to pick the case up.
