UNSEEN CASE S38 · POINT 4

“You’re the bank — just reverse my payment”

I paid £600 to a private car seller yesterday and I’ve changed my mind. You’re the bank — just reverse it.
Hand to a person
Handed to a person

Nadia Sorensen, writing on secure chat, asked the bank to reverse a £600 transfer she made the day before as a car deposit, and wanted the money back that day without a form. The desk escalated the case because her identity was not verified and an instruction to act needs a verified customer. The transfer journal shows the payment as sent and unable to be cancelled, and the desk took no action and has not yet told her anything. A person now needs to pick up the case.
