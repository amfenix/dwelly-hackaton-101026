UNSEEN CASE S42 · POINT 7

Ten events — status question turns into a cancellation

When does my standing order to Fernhill Community Allotments next go out?
Hand to a person
Handed to a person

Priya Langford, a verified customer on the secure portal, asked when her £30 standing order to Fernhill Community Allotments next runs, and later asked to cancel only the 15 December 2025 run while keeping the order active. The desk confirmed the order is active with its next run on 15 December 2025, but its submit request was refused because its facts still showed no confirmation and a status-type request, so it escalated the case. Nothing has been cancelled; a person still has to check her portal confirmation and, if it is valid, cancel only the 15 December run.
