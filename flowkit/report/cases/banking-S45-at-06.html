Decision: Reply

Henrietta Marsh, who was not verified, asked over secure chat whether her €2,600 transfer to Atelier Rousset was still stuck with the other bank or had moved on. The desk checked the payment record, which shows the transfer as sent but with no confirmation that the recipient bank received it, and replied to her with exactly that, noting that no new transfer was made. No trace or escalation was opened, so if the atelier still has nothing, any further step is left for a person.
