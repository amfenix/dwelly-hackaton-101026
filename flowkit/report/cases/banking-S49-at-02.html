UNSEEN CASE S49 · POINT 2

Three requests in one message

Three things: when does my standing order to my sister next go out, why was I charged £4.50 on 1 December, and please change my address to 14 Quillan Road — I’ve moved.
Send for confirmation
Decision: Send for confirmation

Declan Ashworth, who is not verified, wrote in secure chat asking when his standing order to Saoirse next goes out, why he was charged £4.50 on 1 December, and for an address change. The desk treated the message as a change request, and because changes must be confirmed by the customer in the secure portal, it sent him a confirmation request for the standing order change (amount 4.50) and closed the case. A later attempt to look up the recurring system was refused because the case had already closed, so the payment date and the £4.50 charge were never looked up, and no address change was made, all of which is left for a person.
