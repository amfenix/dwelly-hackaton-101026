Behind the reportSources, scoring & what this shows+

A policy, not a promise.

Each public case is cut at every point of its history; the desk sees only the events up to that point and decides the bank's next step. The bank's actual next step is read from the next event (evals/score_points.py); the agent's own notes to a case record are not scored.
